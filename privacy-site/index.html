Privacy Policy

MemoLens helps you save personal memories with photos, captions, moods, tags, and optional voice notes. This policy explains what information the app uses and how your saved memories are handled.

Last updated: 30 June 2026
Version: 1.2.3

What MemoLens Does

MemoLens lets you sign in with Google, add photos from your camera or library, write or dictate captions, record optional voice notes, add moods and tags, mark favorites, search saved memories, and share selected memories to other apps.

Information We Use

When you sign in with Google, the app may access:

  • Your Google account ID
  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile photo

This is used only to show your signed-in account inside the app.

What Stays On Your Device

This version of MemoLens stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Saved image copies
  • Saved voice notes
  • Captions
  • Moods
  • Tags
  • Favorite state
  • Signed-in profile state

MemoLens does not upload your saved memory content to a custom backend in this version of the app. If you use the same Google account on another phone, your saved memories will not automatically appear there.

Permissions

Permissions are requested only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera permission to capture a new photo
  • Photo library permission to choose an existing photo
  • Microphone permission to dictate a caption or record a voice note
  • Speech recognition permission to convert voice into text

Sharing

When you tap Share, the selected photo, voice note, or memory text may be passed to the destination app you choose. That destination app handles the shared content under its own policies and practices.

Ads and Selling Data

MemoLens does not show ads, and we do not sell your personal information or saved memory content.

Data Retention

Memory data stays on your device until you delete memories, clear local memories in Settings, or uninstall MemoLens.

Account and Data Deletion

MemoLens does not keep a custom server-side account database or upload saved memory content to a custom backend in this version of the app.

You can delete or disconnect your data in these ways:

  • Delete an individual memory from the memory detail screen.
  • Open Profile and Settings in MemoLens, then choose Clear local memories to remove saved local memories.
  • Choose Sign out in Profile and Settings to remove the signed-in profile state from the app.
  • To request help with account or data deletion, email bmwdeveloper369@gmail.com.

Deleted local memories are removed from that device and cannot be restored by the MemoLens developer.

Third-Party Services

MemoLens uses Google Sign-In for authentication. Google may process account information according to Google’s own terms and privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the MemoLens developer at bmwdeveloper369@gmail.com.
